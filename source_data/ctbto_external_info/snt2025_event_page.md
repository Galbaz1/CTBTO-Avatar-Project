# SnT2025 Conference – CTBTO Conferences Portal (Event 30)

Source URL: https://conferences.ctbto.org/event/30/

---

The **Preparatory Commission** for the **Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty Organization (CTBTO)** relies on innovation to enhance the capabilities of the Treaty’s verification regime.

SnT2025 will gather **1000+** participants and is scheduled for **8–12 September 2025** at **Hofburg Palace, Vienna** and online.

### Scientific Objectives (as listed)
- Improve nuclear-test-ban monitoring and on-site inspections.
- Support national needs and CTBT universalization.
- Integrate diverse communities and enhance diversity.
- Promote civil and scientific applications and capacity building.

### Key Dates
- **16 Sep 2024:** Registration opens
- **7 Oct 2024:** Abstract submission opens
- **14 Jan 2025:** Abstract submission / financial support deadline
- **31 Aug 2025:** Upload deadline for presentation material
