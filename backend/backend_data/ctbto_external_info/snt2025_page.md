# SnT2025 – CTBT: Science and Technology Conference 2025 (Overview)

Source URL: https://ctbto.org/SnT2025

---

The **Preparatory Commission** for the **Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty Organization (CTBTO)** relies on innovation to enhance the capabilities of the Treaty’s verification regime as well as to help move the Treaty closer to universalization and entry into force.

SnT2025 is the **eighth event** in the CTBT: Science and Technology Conference series and will bring together well over **1000 scientists, technologists, academics, students, and delegates** from the CTBTO’s policy-making organs.

### Venue & Format
- **Location:** Hofburg Palace, Vienna, Austria
- **Dates:** 8–12 September 2025
- **Format:** Hybrid (in-person and online)

### Scientific Objectives
1. Identify methods and technologies that can improve nuclear-test-ban monitoring and on-site inspections.
2. Highlight how scientific developments support national needs and policy objectives in support of the CTBT.
3. Connect, inspire and integrate diverse communities and enhance diversity across gender, geography and generations.
4. Promote civil and scientific applications, capacity building, mentoring and skills development.

### Key Deadlines
- **16 Sep 2024:** Registration opens
- **7 Oct 2024:** Abstract submission opens
- **14 Jan 2025:** Deadline for abstracts & financial support applications
- **31 Aug 2025:** Deadline for uploading presentation materials
