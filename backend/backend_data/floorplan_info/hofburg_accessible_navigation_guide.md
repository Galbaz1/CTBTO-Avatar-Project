# Hofburg Palace SnT2025 Accessible Navigation Guide
## Your Complete Non-Visual Wayfinding Companion

*This guide is optimized for screen readers and follows WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards. All directions use clock-face references and consistent left/right orientations from your perspective.*

---

## 1. Welcome Orientation: Where You Are Now

You are currently standing at the **information kiosk in the Entry Foyer** of Hofburg Palace Vienna. This is your **home base** and primary reference point for all navigation.

### 1.1 Your Immediate Surroundings (Clock-Face Reference)
- **12 o'clock (directly ahead)**: Main conference area entrance, 30 meters straight ahead
- **2 o'clock (front-right)**: Registration desk, 15 meters away - you'll hear staff voices and paper shuffling
- **3 o'clock (directly right)**: Point of Contact desk and cloakroom, 20 meters - listen for coat hangers
- **6 o'clock (behind you)**: Main entrance doors to Schlossplatz square - you'll feel cool air from outside
- **9 o'clock (directly left)**: Corridor leading to ground floor exhibition areas, 25 meters

### 1.2 Immediate Tactile and Audio Landmarks
- **Flooring**: You're standing on polished marble - smooth, cool surface with slight echo
- **Acoustics**: High-ceilinged space with moderate reverberation
- **Background sounds**: Gentle hum of HVAC, occasional footsteps on marble, muffled voices from registration
- **Air flow**: Slight draft from main entrance behind you

## 2. Ground Floor (Parterre) Navigation Routes

### 2.1 Route to Exhibition Areas and Prinz Eugen Saal
**Starting from kiosk, facing toward registration (2 o'clock position):**

1. **Turn 90 degrees left** to face 9 o'clock direction
2. **Walk straight 25 meters** along the marble floor - maintain contact with left wall
3. **Entrance to exhibition area**: Large archway opening - you'll notice:
   - Floor changes from marble to carpeted surface
   - Sound dampening - voices become clearer, less echo
   - Gentle warmth from exhibition lighting

**Within Exhibition Area (Gardehalle I):**
- **Immediate left (15 meters)**: OSI exhibition booths - listen for interactive displays
- **Center area (20-30 meters ahead)**: CTBTO procurement booths and partner exhibitions
- **Far left corner (40 meters)**: Virtual reality corner - distinctive electronic humming
- **Right side (30 meters)**: Forum entrance - described in Section 2.2
- **Far end (50 meters straight)**: **Prinz Eugen Saal** entrance
  - Large wooden double doors
  - Brass handles at 1.1 meters height
  - Capacity: 200 persons, theater-style seating
  - **Events**: Technical oral sessions (O3.1, O3.5, O4.4)

### 2.2 Route to Forum
**From exhibition area, Prinz Eugen Saal is on your left, Forum on your right:**

1. **From center of exhibition area**, turn to face the **right wall**
2. **Walk 20 meters** toward carpeted corridor
3. **Forum entrance**: 
   - Medium-sized room, capacity 150 persons
   - Classroom-style seating arrangement
   - **Events**: Oral sessions (O3.2, O3.6), specialized presentations
   - **Audio characteristics**: Less reverberant than Prinz Eugen Saal
   - Door: Standard width, wood veneer, push-to-open

### 2.3 Essential Ground Floor Services

**Restrooms** (from kiosk):
1. **Face 12 o'clock** (toward main conference area)
2. **Walk 15 meters straight** until you reach the **base of grand staircase**
3. **Turn right** - restrooms are **5 meters** along the right wall
4. **Tactile indicators**: Textured floor tiles 2 meters before entrances
5. **Audio signals**: Accessible stalls have audible door locks

**Prayer Room and Nursing Room** (from kiosk):
1. **Follow route toward exhibition area** (Section 2.1, steps 1-3)
2. **Immediately after entering exhibition area, turn left**
3. **Walk 10 meters along left wall**
4. **Prayer room**: First door, marked with Braille signage
5. **Nursing room**: Second door, 3 meters further, equipped with comfortable seating

**Barrier-Free Elevator to Upper Level** (from exhibition area):
1. **Walk to far end of Gardehalle I** (50 meters from exhibition entrance)
2. **Turn right at end wall**
3. **Elevator**: 15 meters ahead on right side
4. **Audio signals**: Chimes on arrival, voice announcement of floors
5. **Button panel**: Braille markings, tactile floor indicators

## 3. Upper Level (Mezzanine) Navigation Routes

### 3.1 Arrival and Orientation at Top of Feststiege
**When you exit the elevator or reach the top of the grand staircase:**

You are now in the **upper foyer area** with additional exhibition space.

**Immediate landmarks at stair/elevator top:**
- **Flooring**: Transition from marble steps to carpeted exhibition area
- **12 o'clock (straight ahead)**: Corridor leading to main conference rooms (25 meters)
- **10 o'clock (front-left)**: CTBTO Public Information booth - listen for informational videos
- **2 o'clock (front-right)**: YPN booth and networking area
- **3 o'clock (direct right)**: NDCs4All booth with post station - you'll hear paper rustling

### 3.2 Route to Festsaal (Main Conference Hall)
**From top of stairs/elevator:**

1. **Walk straight ahead (12 o'clock) for 25 meters** through upper foyer
2. **Enter main conference corridor** - you'll notice:
   - Carpet changes to traditional palace runner (slightly thicker, with pattern)
   - Sound becomes more muffled due to tapestries and historical furnishings
3. **Continue straight 20 meters** until you reach **large double doors on your left**
4. **Festsaal entrance**: 
   - **Magnificent imperial ballroom**, capacity 1,400 persons
   - **Ornate double doors**: Heavy, carved wood with brass fittings
   - **Handle height**: 1.2 meters, requires firm pull to open
   - **Acoustic profile**: Highly reverberant space with 3-second echo
   - **Seating**: Mix of theater-style and round tables
   - **Assistive listening**: T-coil compatible system available
   - **Events**: High-level plenary (Sep 9), keynotes, panel discussions, closing plenary

### 3.3 Route to Wintergarten
**From Festsaal, continuing in same direction:**

1. **Exit Festsaal and continue straight** in corridor for **15 meters**
2. **Wintergarten entrance**: Next major door on left side
3. **Room characteristics**:
   - **Capacity**: 300 persons, seminar-style layout
   - **Natural lighting**: Large windows (you may feel warmth from sun)
   - **Acoustics**: Moderate reverberation, good for presentations
   - **Events**: Technical presentations and workshops

### 3.4 Route to Zeremoniensaal (E-Poster Area)
**From Wintergarten, continue in corridor:**

1. **Walk 20 meters further** along main corridor
2. **Zeremoniensaal entrance**: Large opening on left side
3. **E-Poster area characteristics**:
   - **Layout**: Open gallery space with poster displays
   - **Flooring**: Polished parquet - you'll hear sharper footstep echoes
   - **Navigation**: Poster boards arranged in parallel rows, 2-meter aisles
   - **Tactile navigation**: Rope barriers guide movement between poster sections
   - **Audio descriptions**: QR codes link to audio abstracts for each poster

### 3.5 Right Side Facilities (Lounge and Services)
**From any main corridor position, turning right leads to:**

**Catering and Lounge Area** (from Festsaal):
1. **Exit Festsaal, turn right** across corridor
2. **Walk 10 meters** to lounge entrance
3. **Facilities**: 
   - Coffee stations - listen for espresso machine sounds
   - Seating areas with soft furnishings
   - Networking zones with cocktail tables

**Information Stations**:
- Located every 30 meters along right corridor wall
- Touch-screen kiosks with audio output
- Staffed help desks during peak hours

**Work Areas**:
- Quiet zones with individual desks
- Power outlets and Wi-Fi workstations
- Located in alcoves off main corridor

**Second Barrier-Free Elevator** (Seitenhalle):
1. **From any right-side facility, walk to end of corridor** (50 meters)
2. **Turn right into Seitenhalle** (side hall)
3. **Elevator**: 20 meters ahead on right wall

## 4. Emergency and Safety Information

### 4.1 Emergency Evacuation Routes (Non-Visual)
**From Ground Floor**: 
- **Primary exit**: Return to main entrance (Section 1.1, 6 o'clock direction)
- **Secondary exit**: Through exhibition area to emergency exits (follow wall-mounted emergency audio beacons)

**From Upper Level**:
- **Primary route**: Feststiege grand staircase - locate by following main corridor back toward elevator/stairs
- **Backup route**: Emergency staircase in Seitenhalle - follow corridor to right side elevator, continue 30 meters past elevator

### 4.2 Emergency Assistance
- **Emergency contact points**: Available at all information stations
- **Staff identification**: SnT2025 staff wear distinctive lanyards with tactile identification badges
- **Emergency assembly point**: Schlossplatz square (outside main entrance)

## 5. Color-Coding and Wayfinding Systems

### 5.1 Audio-Described Color Coding
While visual color coding exists, **all rooms have corresponding audio announcements**:
- **Red indicators**: Radionuclide sessions (Forum, Prinz Eugen Saal)
- **Blue indicators**: Seismic and monitoring sessions (Festsaal, Wintergarten)
- **Green indicators**: Panel discussions and networking (Festsaal, lounge areas)

### 5.2 Assistive Technology Integration
- **Audio beacons**: Bluetooth-enabled markers at major intersections
- **Mobile app compatibility**: SnT2025 app includes audio navigation features
- **QR audio codes**: Posted outside each room for audio descriptions
